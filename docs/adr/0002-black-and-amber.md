# Black with one amber accent

**Status: superseded by [ADR-0008](0008-monochrome-white-accent.md).** The owner did not like the orange on screen.

The palette is near-black, marble white text, and a single amber accent (`#E8A33D`). The owner chose amber over darkroom red and over teal.

Amber ties the two ideas of the site together: a marble Bust in a museum, and a darkroom. Black-and-white photo paper really is developed under amber safelights, so the Darkroom's light is amber too, not red.

## Consequences

- Dark only. No light theme and no toggle for it.
- Amber stays rare: hover, active nav, the Darkroom light, Stack hover. Never a large fill.
